# Gaspard Hedde — Creative Developer
>   ·  [gaspardhedde.fr](https://gaspardhedde.fr)

![gaspardhedde.fr](../../screenshots/gaspardheddefr.png)

## What it is
The portfolio of Gaspard Hedde, a creative developer and Gobelins Paris student who builds websites, apps and immersive experiences. The site is itself a demo of that craft — a minimal frame around an interactive, WebGL-style project showcase.

## What it shows
A pale grey canvas, almost empty by design: a thin 'GASPARD HEDDE' wordmark top-left, social links (CONTACT / LINKEDIN / TWITTER/X / GITHUB) top-right, a fanned 3D-ish stack of colorful project thumbnails floating in the upper right, a 'SNAKE — LIST' view toggle, and a live 'PARIS 17:27:16' clock with a one-line bio bottom-left.

## How it works
A fixed single screen (no scroll, body overflow hidden) where the work lives in an interactive image stack rendered on a <canvas> — you browse projects spatially through the fanned deck, and a SNAKE/LIST toggle switches between the playful stacked view and a plain list. Immersive and motion-led rather than document-like.

## How it's designed
Swiss-minimal meets creative-dev showcase: a light grey ground (#f3f3f3), ultra-thin Helvetica (weight 100) for all type, and near-total emptiness so the colorful, slightly glitchy project deck and the ticking Paris clock become the focus. The restraint frames the WebGL interaction as the hero — the layout gets out of the way.

## Design data
- **Palette:** Light grey ground (#f3f3f3), black text, white; saturated color only in the project-thumbnail stack.
- **Fonts:** Helvetica (thin, weight 100)
