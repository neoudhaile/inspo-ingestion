# AI Lead Generation Agency for B2B Companies | SynergyScale Solutions
> Get 10+ qualified meetings in the next 30 days, or your money back.  ·  [synergyscale.co](https://synergyscale.co/)

![synergyscale.co](../../screenshots/synergyscaleco.png)

## What it is
The landing page for SynergyScale, an AI-powered B2B lead-generation agency run by Dimitar Angelov (the tweet's author). It offers done-for-you cold email, LinkedIn outreach, and appointment setting on a money-back guarantee.

## What it shows
A guarantee-led hero ('Get 10+ qualified meetings in the next 30 days, or your money back') with a stats dashboard mockup (meetings booked, reply rate, revenue, a bar chart) and a client-logo strip (Foxsy, Wisp, JBB Capital, LeadGen). Below: 'We handle the machine. You take the meetings.', a 14-day onboarding timeline, a 'Numbers that speak for themselves' metrics block, a founder note from Dimitar, testimonials, a 'SynergyScale vs. the old way' comparison, a 'who this is for / isn't' qualifier, and an FAQ — all funneling to a Calendly discovery call.

## How it works
A very long single-page funnel (~15.5k px) built as a self-hosted app with scroll-triggered reveals and a subtle animated canvas backdrop in the hero. Every CTA ('Claim a spot', 'Book a Call', 'Talk to Dimitar') points to the same Calendly link; a top scarcity bar reinforces urgency.

## How it's designed
Modern dark SaaS-agency look: near-black canvas (rgb(3,7,18)), white text, and a blue glow gradient behind the hero. Headings use a heavy 72px/800 Plus Jakarta Sans with blue-highlighted key phrases, while body copy is Inter — Next.js-style self-hosted fonts (note the 'Inter Fallback' / 'Plus Jakarta Sans Fallback' families). Heavy use of inline SVG (144) for icons, dashboard glyphs, and the comparison/checklist graphics; one canvas drives the ambient hero animation.
