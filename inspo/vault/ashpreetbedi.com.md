# Ashpreet Bedi
> Founder & CEO of Agno. I write about software, AI, and the systems we build around them.  ·  [ashpreetbedi.com](https://www.ashpreetbedi.com/)

![ashpreetbedi.com](../../screenshots/ashpreetbedicom.png)

## What it is
The personal blog / portfolio of Ashpreet Bedi, founder & CEO of Agno, collecting his essays on software, AI, and the systems we build around them. The tweet shares his article 'Systems Engineering: Building Agentic Software That Works'; the site root is what resolves.

## What it shows
A dark home page with a one-line bio ('Founder & CEO of Agno...'), a reverse-chronological article list — 'Image search using text classification', 'Agent Platform That Builds Itself', 'Auto-Improving Software' — each with a date, dek and 'READ ARTICLE' link, an 'ALL WRITING' link, a 'STAY UP TO DATE' email-subscribe block, and a footer carrying a light/dark toggle, a 'BUILDING CODA' status, and GitHub/X/LinkedIn/RSS icons.

## How it works
A Tailwind v4 static site (the :root is full of Tailwind's oklch design tokens) with everything self-hosted — no third-party script or stylesheet hosts beyond ashpreetbedi.com. It ships a command-palette search (Cmd+K), uses 13 inline SVG icons and no raster images, and scrolls a modest single column (~1203px on desktop).

## How it's designed
A warm dark 'paper' aesthetic: the background is --color-paper #1a1816 (rendered rgb(26,24,22)) with off-white --color-ink #f4f4f5 text and a coral-red --color-accent #f87171 on links and 'READ ARTICLE'. Two custom faces do the work — aeonik for sans body and aeonikFono, a monospace, for headings and the uppercase tracked micro-labels — giving it a deliberate developer-journal feel.
