## What it is
The homepage of Arnesh Banerjee (@Arnesh_24), the tweet's author — an undergraduate researcher (B.Tech CSE, Data Science) at Heritage Institute of Technology, Kolkata, working on multi-agent reinforcement learning, computer vision and AI safety.

## What it shows
A classic academic CV page: a bio ('I like to train models, build agents, and tinker with multi-agent systems…'), a top anchor nav (About, Experience, Publications, Preprints, Ongoing, Blogs, Achievements) with CV / GitHub / LinkedIn / Letters / Email links, then Research Experience, Publications, Preprints, Ongoing Research, Blogs and Achievements sections.

## How it works
About as barebones as the web gets — hand-written static HTML with no framework, no CSS custom properties, no webfonts and no analytics scripts at all. A single image, no svg/canvas/video, 3,485px tall, constrained to an 800px reading column.

## How it's designed
Pure browser-default 'researcher homepage' aesthetic. From the DOM it's a white background with black text rendered in the default serif stack (Times / Times New Roman / Georgia), a 34px/700 serif h1, and unstyled default-blue links (rgb(0,0,238)). Content over chrome — the academic-LaTeX-homepage look, with no design system to speak of.
