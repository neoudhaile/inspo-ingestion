# Motion (prev Framer Motion): JavaScript & React animation library
> The animation library loved by humans and agents alike.  ·  [motion.dev](https://motion.dev/)

## What it is
Home page of Motion, the animation library formerly known as Framer Motion, covering React, JavaScript and Vue, plus its paid Motion+ tier.

## What it shows
Live animation demos, a 'Built with Motion' showcase, latest posts, and starting-point guides and examples. The pitch is small APIs and expressive interfaces, aimed at both developers and AI agents.

## How it works
A long, roughly 12,770px scrolling marketing page with 15 looping <video> clips, 59 images and a canvas. Analytics come from a self-hosted analytics.motion.dev, and Paddle and ProfitWell handle Motion+ billing. No SPA framework global was detected in the DOM probe.

## How it's designed
Near-black teal-tinted canvas (--background #0d1111, body rgb(14,19,18)) with off-white text (#ededec) and a restrained pale accent (#e8eeee). A green Motion+ accent (oklch .76 .15 155) and lavender --accent-3 (#a5b4fc) appear sparingly. The h1 is 34.56px TASA Orbiter 700, with Geist Mono for kicker/code detail. CSS vars define diagonal 'dossier' stripe patterns and wide kicker tracking.
