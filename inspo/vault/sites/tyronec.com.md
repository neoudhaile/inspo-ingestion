# Tyrone C. | Product Designer for Startups & Agencies
> Germany-based product designer helping startups and agencies ship polished mobile apps and Framer websites.  ·  [tyronec.com](https://www.tyronec.com/)

![tyronec.com](../../../screenshots/tyroneccom.png)

**What it is** — The dark portfolio of Tyrone C., whose h1 reads 'Hi, i'm Tyrone, a product designer based in germany partnering with startups and agencies to turn products into unforgettable experiences.' The tweet showcases client brand explorations rather than this site directly; the site is his portfolio and the primary link surfaced from the tweet metadata.

**What it shows** — A fixed left sidebar (the intro, a 'Services' list — Product Design Retainer, Framer Design Sprint, Framer Development — plus Socials, a live local time and Send Email / Contact-on-X buttons) beside a right-hand gallery of case studies with 'Product Design' and 'Framer Websites' tabs: a posture/fitness app 'Phy' (Postural Age 27.5, top imbalances), a SaaS dashboard on a warm gradient, iOS reminder/to-do apps (WWDC '24), a macOS list app, a media player, a 'Customisable Package $1999,89' pricing card and a crypto/finance wallet app (Total Balance $344,334.89).

**How it works** — A Framer site (framework global exposed; assets from framerusercontent.com and events.framer.com) with no separate analytics beyond Framer's own events endpoint. It is media-light in markup — 9 <img>, 4 <svg>, 0 canvas/video and 1 <iframe> — over a 5,242px page; the case-study visuals are image compositions rather than live embeds.

**How it's designed** — A confident all-dark, product-first layout. getComputedStyle reports a pure-black body background rgb(0,0,0) with a white h1 rgb(255,255,255) set in Inter, and links render in classic web-blue rgb(0,0,238). Type is Inter / Inter Variable (loaded 400) throughout, with Geist declared but unloaded. The only colour comes from the soft gradient cards the app mockups sit on; the surrounding UI chrome stays strictly monochrome. No colour :root custom properties are exposed (only a corner-shape flag), so colours are read from the rendered page.

**Palette** — Pure-black canvas (#000) with white text and classic-blue links (#0000ee); colour arrives only via the gradient backdrops behind the app mockups.
**Fonts** — Inter / Inter Variable (loaded, 400) for everything; Geist declared but unloaded — no other webfont payload.
