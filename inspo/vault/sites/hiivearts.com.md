# Hiive Arts
> 100+ Opportunities/mo — how we help B2B businesses implement multi-channel GTM systems.  ·  [hiivearts.com](https://hiivearts.com/x)

![hiivearts.com](../../../screenshots/hiiveartscom.png)

**What it is** — A stripped-down, single-screen landing page for Hiive Arts, a B2B go-to-market agency that builds multi-channel outbound systems and pitches '100+ opportunities per month' for its partners. The page exists to do one thing: get you to book a call.

**What it shows** — A centered hero — 'HIIVE ARTS' wordmark at top-left, a black 'Book a Call' pill at top-right, a green-dot 'Now accepting new partners' status pill, an oversized '100+ Opportunities/mo' headline and a one-line subhead. The centerpiece is a large embedded Wistia video — a Loom-style screen recording titled 'How this works' that outlines Build Infrastructure (1500–3000 emails/day, 50–250 LinkedIn messages/day), Find Contacts and Develop Messaging, with a circular talking-head bubble in the corner. A round scroll-down chevron sits below; the footer is just a copyright line and an email.

**How it works** — A pure single-CTA funnel: every path leads to the cal.com booking embed behind 'Book a Call.' The Wistia video carries the actual explanation, and the scroll chevron hints at content below the fold (largely empty in the capture). Sentry is wired in for client error monitoring.

**How it's designed** — Aggressively minimal and high-contrast. A plain white ground holds near-black Inter typography — a heavy 44px/800 headline does the shouting — with a single black pill as the only chrome and a green status dot as the only color. Whitespace centers everything on the video, letting the bold number and the recording sell the service with zero decoration.

**Palette** — White ground (#fff) with black text and a black CTA pill (#000); a single green status dot — effectively monochrome.
**Fonts** — Inter across the board (weights 300–900, via Google Fonts); the embedded Wistia player ships its own bundled font (WistiaPlayerInter).
