# Satyam Namdev | AI Engineer
> Backend AI Engineer @ezAIx — building production AI systems, RAG pipelines, and multi-agent orchestration.  ·  [spyrosigma.in](https://spyrosigma.in)

![spyrosigma.in](../../../screenshots/spyrosigmain.png)

**What it is** — The personal portfolio of Satyam Namdev (handle 'spyrosigma'), the builder behind Zendex. The meta pitch is 'Backend AI Engineer @ezAIx — building production AI systems, RAG pipelines, and multi-agent orchestration.' It's a single-page résumé/portfolio leading with his name as the h1.

**What it shows** — A warm-paper, terminal-flavoured one-pager: a 'Satyam Namdev' hero, then stacked sections — Experience (ezAIx, Tunable Labs, each with 'read more →' detail pages), Projects (MedMitra with a live link + GitHub, StreetSpecter), and Skills. Outbound links go to x.com/Spyrosigma, GitHub, and a 'Say hi' mailto.

**How it works** — Anchor nav (#experience / #projects / #skills) scrolls the single page; experience entries deep-link to sub-pages (/experience/ezaix, /experience/ezaix/extras, /experience/tunablelabs) and projects link out to live demos (medmitra.vercel.app) and GitHub repos. No framework global is exposed and all JS/CSS is self-hosted.

**How it's designed** — A deliberate monospace 'engineer's notebook' aesthetic: a warm cream ground (--background #f7f7f2 rendered rgb(247,247,242)) with near-black ink (--foreground #111) and a single terracotta accent (--accent #d4541a), plus muted gray (--muted #5c5c5c) and a hairline border (--border #e2e2da) on white cards (--card-bg #fff). Type is exclusively GeistMono (the only loaded webfont; no sans, no serif), which gives the whole page a code-editor feel. Zero raster images — all iconography is inline SVG (37 svg, 0 img) — keeping it light and crisp. h1 renders at 30px on both desktop and mobile.

**Palette** — Warm cream paper #f7f7f2 with near-black ink #111, muted gray #5c5c5c, hairline border #e2e2da, white cards, and a single terracotta accent #d4541a.
**Fonts** — GeistMono only (monospace), used for all text; no sans or serif loaded.
