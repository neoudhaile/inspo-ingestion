# Notion Website Maker: Create A Free Website Built In Notion
> Turn your Notion page into your website in 1 minute — free, no-code, no credit card.  ·  [simple.ink](https://www.simple.ink/)

![simple.ink](../../../screenshots/simpleink.png)

**What it is** — A no-code SaaS that turns any Notion page into a live, custom-domain website in one click. The pitch is right there in the hero — 'Build a website with Notion' — and it's freemium with no credit card to start. The audience is Notion power-users who already keep their content in Notion and want it published as a real site (portfolios, help centers, job boards, landing pages) without touching code.

**What it shows** — A long (~15.5k px) marketing landing page. It opens on a hero with a browser-frame product mockup, then walks through 'Your Notion Website in 1 Minute' and 'Hassle-Free Notion Websites' value sections, a wall of real customer sites (Kylar San Juan's portfolio, a 'Jobs @ Handle' board, a 'LiveTechHelper' help center, several personal portfolios) shown in embeds, testimonials, and a deep SEO/CMS FAQ ('Can you use Notion as a CMS?', 'Is Notion SEO friendly?') before a final 'Start building your Notion website for free' CTA.

**How it works** — A conventional scrolling marketing page driven by repeated 'Start free' / 'Build a website' CTAs. Five iframes embed live example Notion sites inline. A Hellobar promo strip rides the top ('Welcome back 🤗'), a Zendesk widget offers chat, and FirstPromoter runs affiliate/referral tracking — the funnel machinery of a self-serve SaaS. On mobile the nav collapses behind a hamburger.

**How it's designed** — Built on Webflow (all assets served from cdn.prod.website-files.com, a single Webflow stylesheet). Notably it ships no brand webfont at all — the only loaded face is 'webflow-icons', so every word renders in the native system-ui stack; the big h1 is system-ui 54px/800 in near-black (#222) on white. Personality comes from a friendly multi-accent brand palette declared in CSS vars — two blues (#0f93ff, #1a61fb), a UI purple (#7970ff), a green (#2eb67c), a peach (#ff8f7d) and a yellow (#ffe871) — used across icons, the browser mockups and section accents. Heavy on imagery (56 imgs, 80 inline SVGs) and product embeds rather than illustration.

**Palette** — White ground, near-black text (#222); friendly multi-accent brand set — blue #0f93ff/#1a61fb, purple #7970ff, green #2eb67c, peach #ff8f7d, yellow #ffe871.
**Fonts** — System UI stack only — no brand webfont payload (only a 'webflow-icons' icon font loads).
