# Tobias van Schneider — House of van Schneider (HOVS), a creative-direction studio & portfolio
> I create, therefore I am.  ·  [vanschneider.com](https://vanschneider.com/)

![vanschneider.com](../../../screenshots/vanschneidercom.png)

**What it is** — The portfolio and studio site of Tobias van Schneider — 'House of van Schneider' (HOVS), his New-York-based creative-direction studio 'that builds and runs its own products — and occasionally works with partners such as NASA/JPL to brand their space missions.' He is the maker growing mymind and the co-founder of Semplice and Carbonmade. This is the site the mymind 'Vibe Search' tweet resolves to via the export metadata — the author's own portfolio.

**What it shows** — An enormous (34,491px) single-page editorial homepage. It opens on a black hero: a red ✣HOVS logo, a fine cathedral-arch line motif, an engraving of two cherubs holding a 'Crea Ergo Sum' banner, and a towering white headline 'I CREATE; THEREFORE I AM / DESIGN PRAXIS EST. 2000.' A tri-column magazine nav groups links under REPERTOIRE (essentials, essays, the weekly), CRÉATIVITÉ (mixtapes, books, quotes) and NARRATIVE (the praxis, artifacts, photo archive). Below sits an intro ('Currently growing mymind™… also co-founder of Semplice™ and Carbonmade™'), Featured Essays and case work (e.g. 'HOVS — a retro-futuristic interpretation of the original BMW airhead,' 'NASA — Branding NASA's mission to Jupiter's icy moon, Europa'), closing on 'Thank you for visiting the house.'

**How it works** — A WordPress site built on van Schneider's own Semplice page-builder — the DOM is saturated with WordPress block presets (--wp--preset--color / gradient / spacing / shadow tokens) and jQuery, plus a large custom easing library (--ease-*-* cubic-beziers) driving its scroll/hover animation. Analytics run through Google Tag Manager and Google Fonts is linked, though the real display type is self-hosted. It is heavily media-driven: 147 images, 12 inline <video> clips and 105 inline SVGs across a 34k-pixel scroll, and it links out to his products — mymind.com (three times, 'Visit mymind.com'), semplice.com and carbonmade.com.

**How it's designed** — Bold monochrome editorial with a single red accent. The stage renders near-black (a .column-content wrapper paints rgb(45,45,45); the <body> itself is transparent) with white type, and the brand accent is a saturated red — the red ✣HOVS logo and the red favicon, matching the site's own --wp--preset--color--vivid-red #cf2e2e. Display type is MaisonNeue (Book / Demi / Bold / Medium / Light, self-hosted) — the hero 'I CREATE; THEREFORE I AM' is MaisonNeue-Bold at ~130px — paired with his commissioned serif 'Tobias' (Tobias-Regular) and EngraversGothic-Bold for small-caps labels; muted sage-green group labels (REPERTOIRE / CRÉATIVITÉ / NARRATIVE) provide the only other colour. (Merriweather and Inter are declared but unloaded.)

**Palette** — Near-black stage (wrapper rgb(45,45,45)) with white type and one saturated brand red (#cf2e2e — the ✣HOVS logo & red favicon); muted sage-green nav labels as the lone secondary.
**Fonts** — MaisonNeue (Book/Demi/Bold/Medium/Light) for display & UI, his own 'Tobias' serif (Tobias-Regular) and EngraversGothic-Bold for small-caps labels — all self-hosted; Merriweather and Inter declared but unloaded.
