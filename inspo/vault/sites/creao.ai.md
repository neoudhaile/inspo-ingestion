# CREAO AI │ Agents that run your business
> CREAO builds AI agents that run your work — they plug into your existing tools, learn how you operate, and compound your knowledge around the clock.  ·  [creao.ai](https://creao.ai/)

![creao.ai](../../../screenshots/creaoai.png)

**What it is** — The marketing homepage for CREAO, an AI-agent platform that sells 'agents that run your business.' The whole long-scroll page is a pitch: spin up agents tailored to your work that plug into your existing stack and operate 24/7 so 'a week of work' is done 'by Tuesday.'

**What it shows** — A tall single-column landing page opening on the oversized claim 'AI should be running your business by now.' From there it walks the value prop in stacked sections — 'Agents tailored to your work. Running 24/7.', an 'Already plugged into your stack.' integrations grid of app logos, 'Set the cadence. Stay in control.', 'The system learns how you work.', a results block ('A week of work, by Tuesday.' with a 95%+ stat), 'Always at the frontier.', a 'Start free. Scale when ready.' pricing/CTA, a 'Notes from the frontier.' blog teaser and a closing FAQ. It ends on a big 'What should CREAO run for you first?' prompt above the wordmark footer.

**How it works** — A conventional conversion funnel built in Framer: anchored nav links scroll the one document, product features are illustrated with inline mock UI cards and an integrations logo wall, and repeated 'Start free' CTAs push to sign-up. A heavy analytics/ad-pixel layer (Amplitude, GTM, plus LinkedIn/Facebook/Twitter conversion pixels) instruments the page for paid acquisition.

**How it's designed** — Clean, bright SaaS-editorial. A white ground (rgb(255,255,255)) carries near-black text, with one full-bleed dark section breaking the rhythm and soft tinted panels framing the feature mock-ups. Typography pairs a very heavy display sans for headlines — the h1 'AI should be running your business by now.' renders in DM Sans at 80px / weight 900 — against an Inter body, with a Frank Ruhl Libre serif loaded for accent/quote text. Generous whitespace, rounded cards and small inline product screenshots give it the polished, slightly templated feel of a well-funded Framer site.

**Palette** — White ground (rgb(255,255,255)) with near-black text (h1 rgb(22,24,26)); one full-bleed dark section and soft tinted feature panels — restrained, near-monochrome with subtle accent tints.
**Fonts** — DM Sans for the heavy display headlines (h1 80px/900), Inter for body (weights 400/500/700 loaded), and a Frank Ruhl Libre serif loaded for accent text; many other families are declared in Framer's font manifest but left unloaded.
