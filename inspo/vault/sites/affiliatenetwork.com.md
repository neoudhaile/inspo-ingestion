# AffiliateNetwork.com
> The #1 Affiliate Network for organic marketing.  ·  [affiliatenetwork.com](https://affiliatenetwork.com/)

![affiliatenetwork.com](../../../screenshots/affiliatenetworkcom.png)

**What it is** — The marketing site for AffiliateNetwork.com, a performance-based influencer/creator marketing platform — headlined 'The #1 Affiliate Network for organic marketing' and described in meta as 'a performance-based influencer marketing platform that helps brands scale through viral creators, AI-powered campaigns, and guaranteed results.' Bella's (@growthsuck) bookmark has no tweet text, so the site is the entire showcase.

**What it shows** — A dark conversion landing page leading with 'The #1 Affiliate Network for organic marketing' and dual 'Sign up as a brand' / 'Sign up as a Creator' CTAs (plus a yellow 'Are you a Creator?' nav button), followed by oversized trust stats (150k+ creators, 150M+ app downloads, $100M+ revenue generated, 30B+ views, 50M+ engagements, 500K+ posts), 'How it works' and 'Why choose us' sections, a 'Trusted by leading brands worldwide' logo wall, and case-study cards for Rizz App, LooksMax AI and Memix.

**How it works** — Built in Framer: the public domain (affiliatenetwork.com) is a thin shell that embeds the real Framer site via a cross-origin iframe at frame.affiliatenetwork.com — assets load from framerusercontent.com and telemetry from events.framer.com (no Next/Nuxt/Vue/Svelte global). It is heavily vector-driven (149 inline SVGs and 44 images) over a tall ~5541px desktop page, and contact/footer links resolve to socialreachly.com (mailto) and brands.affiliatenetwork.com (terms/privacy).

**How it's designed** — A near-black canvas (rendered rgb(0,0,0)) with light-grey headline text (rgb(222,222,222)) lit by a single electric chartreuse-yellow accent (#f2ff00, rendered rgb(242,255,0)) on the primary buttons (black labels). Type is a stack of geometric sans served by Framer — Inter Display for big headings (hero h1 at 75px), Inter for body, plus DM Sans and Satoshi — giving a modern, high-contrast startup-landing look built around oversized stat numbers and rounded case-study cards.

**Palette** — Black background, light-grey #dedede headlines, electric chartreuse #f2ff00 CTA
**Fonts** — Inter Display + Inter (with DM Sans & Satoshi), Framer-hosted
