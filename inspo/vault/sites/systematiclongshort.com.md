# Systematic Long Short | Substack
> Understand how the game is played at the highest levels  ·  [systematiclongshort.com](https://www.systematiclongshort.com/)

![systematiclongshort.com](../../../screenshots/systematiclongshortcom.png)

**What it is** — The custom-domain home of "Systematic Long Short", a Substack newsletter (h1 "Systematic Long Short") written by an entrepreneur and systematic portfolio manager about quant/hedge-fund investing — "I've sat in the PM seat at pod shops and managed real money at scale... I want to help you understand how the game is played at the highest levels." Over 5,000 subscribers per the on-page count.

**What it shows** — The visit opens on Substack's full-screen subscribe gate: a classical golden-statue avatar, the title, the author's PM-credentials bio, "Over 5,000 subscribers", an email field with an orange Subscribe button, and a "No thanks" dismiss revealing the post archive behind it.

**How it works** — Stock Substack publication on a custom domain — email-capture modal first, dismissible to reach the chronological finance post list; subscriptions and reading are Substack-hosted, with Sentry, Datadog RUM and GTM instrumentation.

**How it's designed** — Near-stock Substack dark theme: a charcoal #171717 web background with Substack's signature brand orange (#FF6719) as the accent/pop color on the Subscribe CTA — i.e. the defaults left in place, contrasted with Dan Koe's customized sage variant. Same type system: Spectral serif for reading, SF Pro Display / system sans for the 24px h1 and UI. Imagery is limited to the statue avatar; it's a text-and-credibility play.

**Palette** — Charcoal canvas (#171717) with Substack default brand orange accent (#FF6719) on the Subscribe CTA; white headings, grey reading text
**Fonts** — Spectral (serif) for reading body; SF Pro Display / system sans for UI + h1
