# Rava Labs — independent design studio
> Rava Labs is an independent design studio partnering with forward-thinking teams.  ·  [ravalabs.com](https://www.ravalabs.com/)

![ravalabs.com](../../../screenshots/ravalabscom.png)

**What it is** — Ravalabs® is the studio of designer Gabriel Obholz (@gabriell_lab), 'an independent studio creating meaningful digital experiences.' It's the author's own studio, linked from his design-engineering tip about velocity-driven stretch + motion blur — and it embodies exactly that grade of motion craft.

**What it shows** — A long portfolio (~18,300px): a hero paragraph 'Rava® partners with visionaries to transform raw ideas into refined designs', a 'Behind Rava Labs' origin story, Selected Clients, an Experiences case-study reel, a Skills block, 'Awards & Recognitions', and a pricing section — a $7,900/month retainer alongside a one-time 'based on project' model and a 7-day, 4-custom-screen sprint. Booking runs through cal.com; prior roles link out to Porsche and Audi.

**How it works** — Built on Framer (framerusercontent.com assets, events.framer.com) with an unpkg-hosted stylesheet. It is very media-dense — 91 img, 11 video, 16 svg — to carry the case-study reels. Primary CTAs route to cal.com/ravalabs/discovery and a mailto to gabrielobholz@gmail.com.

**How it's designed** — Clean Swiss-modern studio look that keeps text restrained and lets the work imagery carry the page. From the DOM the body is white (rgb(255,255,255)) with near-black text (h1 color rgb(23,23,23)). The type system is PP Neue Montreal Book plus the Suisse Intl family (Book 450 / Regular 400 / Bold) grotesques for body and UI, with ARK-ES (a light 300 display serif) as an accent face; the hero h1 renders deliberately small at 23.4px/450 in Suisse Intl Book.

**Palette** — White canvas (rgb(255,255,255)) with near-black ink (rgb(23,23,23)); color comes entirely from the embedded case-study media.
**Fonts** — PP Neue Montreal Book + Suisse Intl (Book 450 / Regular / Bold) grotesques for text, with ARK-ES light serif as a display accent.
