# Ribbit
> Ribbit is a creative motion agency based in Copenhagen founded to create memorable and engaging animations and motion graphics.  ·  [ribbit.dk](https://ribbit.dk/)

![ribbit.dk](../../../screenshots/ribbitdk.png)

**What it is** — The studio site of Ribbit, a 'Curious Fullservice Motion Agency' based in Copenhagen (h1: 'Ribbit is a creative motion agency based in Copenhagen'). The tweet flags it as 'how you create a truly mesmerizing digital experience' — the site itself is the motion showreel.

**What it shows** — A playful hero where bouncy, googly-eyed 3D claymation characters orbit a fat rounded 'ribbit' wordmark, then a featured-project reel (Toiomi, Birdie, 'Hi energy'), a services rundown ('From Idea to Done.' — explainer videos, animated identities) and a step-by-step process block (Script & References, Technical, Deadline & Format, Voiceover & Music) ending in a 'Book a meeting' contact card.

**How it works** — A single ~5,314px scroll where the 'mesmerizing' effect comes from scroll- and hover-driven 3D character animation played back as long image sequences — 758 <img> elements act as animation frames rather than photos, alongside one hero <video>. It's a hand-built vanilla site (no JS framework global), content managed in Prismic (og image on images.prismic.io) with Google Tag Manager for analytics; a rich set of cubic-bezier easing tokens (--sine, --quart-in-out, --expo, --back…) drives the motion.

**How it's designed** — Bold, toylike and tactile: an off-white ground (--lightgrey #FAFAFA, rendered rgb(250,250,250)) with dark-ink body text (--darkink #2F2F2F) so the saturated 3D characters and a candy palette of purples, greens, yellows and oranges (--lightpurple #A88BFA, --midpurple #5B21B6, --yellow #FFD948, --orange #FF9C00, --lightgreen #95C154) carry the personality. The display face is FKT Echo at a massive 240px for the headline, with FKGroteskNeue for everything else, and a dark-brown footer anchors the page.

**Palette** — Off-white ground (#FAFAFA) with dark-ink text (#2F2F2F); a candy accent set of purples/greens/yellows/oranges (#A88BFA, #5B21B6, #FFD948, #FF9C00, #95C154) supplied by the 3D characters, over a dark-brown footer.
**Fonts** — FKT Echo (huge 240px display headline) + FKGroteskNeue (regular/medium/bold body) — all self-hosted and loaded.
