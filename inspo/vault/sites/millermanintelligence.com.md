# Millerman Intelligence — Political Philosophy for Investors, Founders & Institutions
> Political-philosophical intelligence for investors, founders, and institutions.  ·  [millermanintelligence.com](https://millermanintelligence.com/)

![millermanintelligence.com](../../../screenshots/millermanintelligencecom.png)

**What it is** — A single-page editorial site for Dr. Michael Millerman's paid political-philosophy practice — briefings, seminars and research sold to investors, founders, AI organizations and institutions 'navigating technology, sovereignty, liberalism, and modern order.' It reads like a one-man think-tank's prospectus.

**What it shows** — A long broadsheet of a page. The hero states the pitch — 'Political-philosophical intelligence for investors, founders, and institutions' — then 'The old frameworks are not enough.' A numbered 'Recent Work' index lists briefs No.1–7 (AI Charters, Palantir, Civilizationalists, Stargate, Capex Supercycle, SpaceX·Anthropic, Gigawatt), each tagged with month and page count. Below come 'Ways to work with Millerman Intelligence' (formats), 'What we help you think through' (themes), a 'For people with real stakes' section, an 'About Dr. Michael Millerman' block studded with podcast/press appearances (Pompliano, Blockworks, First Things, Hillsdale), 'The Palantir Manifesto, Point by Point,' and a closing 'Request a briefing, seminar, or project conversation' contact form.

**How it works** — Anchor navigation (Briefs / Formats / Themes / About) scrolls the one long document; each brief links to its own /briefs/* page; press mentions link out to YouTube, iHeart and First Things. The primary CTA is 'Request a Private Briefing,' with a contact form and a mailto fallback. A sibling 'Millerman School' and a 'Speaking' subdomain are linked in the footer.

**How it's designed** — Deliberately analog and editorial — it wants to feel like printed intelligence, not a SaaS landing page. A warm paper-cream ground (--bg #f4efe3) carries navy ink (--text #1a2540) with a single oxblood/burgundy accent (--accent #7b2d26), broken by one full-bleed dark-navy section (--bg-dark #1a2540). Typography does the heavy lifting: a large Cormorant Garamond display serif sets the hero h1 (~73.6px, weight 500) over a Source Serif Pro body, with Inter reserved for small UI labels; hairline rules, a narrow ~880px measure and generous whitespace complete the broadsheet feel. It's a hand-built static site (no JS framework detected), served via Cloudflare with Cloudflare Insights.

**Palette** — Paper cream ground (--bg #f4efe3, --bg-alt #faf7ee), navy ink (--text #1a2540), oxblood/burgundy accent (--accent #7b2d26), and one full-bleed dark-navy section (--bg-dark #1a2540).
**Fonts** — Cormorant Garamond (display serif) + Source Serif Pro (body) with Inter for small UI text — all loaded via Google Fonts.
