# Rich Zou — personal site (founder of Bo Le Capital)
> 20 y/o Fuzhounese American. From New York, now based in SF.  ·  [richzou.com](https://richzou.com/)

![richzou.com](../../../screenshots/richzoucom.png)

**What it is** — The personal site of Rich Zou (@richzou) — a 20-year-old Fuzhounese-American writer/founder ('From New York, now based in SF'), founder of Bo Le Capital (a talent-investing fund named after 伯乐 / Bo Le, the Chinese figure who could recognise exceptional horses others overlooked; previously 'talent at xAI'). It's the author's hub behind the bookmarked essay.

**What it shows** — A single-screen, text-only homepage: a lowercase first-person bio ('hey! i'm rich. currently: founder of bo le capital … previously: talent at xai…') with a minimal top nav of just '( Essays )' and '( Restaurants )'. No imagery — pure prose.

**How it works** — A Next.js site (Geist + Geist Mono via next/font; Next-style hashed assets) with essay/OG content stored in Supabase (slelguoygbfzlpylpxfs.supabase.co). Zero media (0 img/canvas/video, 4 svg), a single screen (900px), and no third-party analytics scripts.

**How it's designed** — Deliberate, quiet minimalism. From getComputedStyle the canvas is near-white rgb(250,250,250) with near-black ink (body rgb(26,26,26)) and soft mid-grey links (rgb(60,60,60)); there is no custom-property palette. Type is Geist (variable 100–900) with Geist Mono — the mono most likely carrying the parenthetical '( Essays ) ( Restaurants )' nav labels — for a clean, technical, understated feel.

**Palette** — A near-white canvas (rgb(250,250,250)) with near-black text (rgb(26,26,26)) and mid-grey links (rgb(60,60,60)) — monochrome, no accent.
**Fonts** — Geist (variable 100–900) for text and Geist Mono for the nav/labels.
