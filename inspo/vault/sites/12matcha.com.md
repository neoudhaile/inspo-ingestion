# 12 Matcha — Forever in Pursuit of Exquisite Matcha
>   ·  [12matcha.com](https://12matcha.com)

![12matcha.com](../../../screenshots/12matchacom.png)

**What it is** — The brand and commerce site for 12 Matcha, a matcha and dessert boutique in NoHo, New York. It's a refined direct-to-consumer storefront that leads with mood and craft rather than products — the matcha equivalent of a fashion house homepage.

**What it shows** — A full-bleed photographic hero of an elegant white European-style storefront, overlaid with a centered serif wordmark '12' and the line 'ALWAYS IN PURSUIT'. A minimal top nav (THE 12 APPROACH, THE 12 STORE) and a privacy/cookie banner. Scrolling down (the page is ~4,650px tall) moves through brand story, video, and store sections.

**How it works** — A long, vertical, scroll-driven brand narrative — full-screen photographic and video sections (5 videos) you move through top to bottom, ending in the store. Navigation is sparse and editorial; the experience is about scrolling through atmosphere before you reach commerce. Includes an accessibility menu and a full cookie-consent customiser.

**How it's designed** — Quiet luxury editorial: a near-white/cream and black palette lets large architectural photography carry the page. The display face is a distinctive monospace-serif ('gc16') used for the big 'ALWAYS IN PURSUIT' headline, paired with abcDiatype as a clean grotesque for body — a fashion-editorial pairing that signals taste and restraint. Generous negative space, centered composition, almost no UI chrome.

**Palette** — Cream/near-white (#eeede7, #fff) and near-black (#111) with white text over photography; color comes entirely from the imagery.
**Fonts** — gc16 (monospace-serif display), abcDiatype (grotesque body)
