# Dave Krugman | Photographer & Digital Artist
> Photographer, writer, and digital artist. Creating work at the intersection of technology and art.  ·  [davekrugman.com](https://www.davekrugman.com/)

![davekrugman.com](../../../screenshots/davekrugmancom.png)

**What it is** — The personal site of Dave Krugman — photographer, writer and digital artist (the page's own h1 is simply 'DaveKrugman'). It doubles as a hub for his photography, NFT/digital-art work and his current 'WINDOWS' project.

**What it shows** — A long single-page scroll broken into About, Photography, Digital Art, Get in Touch and 'Latest from socials' sections, with press credits (New York Times, Bloomberg, Decrypt x META) and a live feed of recent X posts — several themed around 'WINDOWS' / '2026 is the year of window'.

**How it works** — A dark, scroll-driven one-pager (~9,950px tall) with anchored nav; two <canvas> elements power the window/glow motif (the CSS exposes --window-amber / --window-amber-glow tokens), and a social feed pulls in his latest tweets. CTAs route to /contact and to Instagram/X.

**How it's designed** — Terminal-meets-editorial dark theme: a near-black ground (#0a0a0c) with warm amber as the signature accent (#ffb84d) offset by cool cyan (#00d4ff), Cousine monospace for body copy and DM Sans for display headings. The monospace plus glowing amber windows read like a darkroom console.

**Palette** — Near-black ground (#0a0a0c), off-white text (#e8e6e3), amber accent (#ffb84d), cyan (#00d4ff) and warm orange (#ff6b35).
**Fonts** — Cousine (monospace) body + DM Sans (sans) headings.
