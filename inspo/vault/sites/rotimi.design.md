# Srotimi: Product Design for Ambitious Tech Startups
> Design Partner For Ambitious Tech Startups.  ·  [rotimi.design](https://www.rotimi.design/)

![rotimi.design](../../../screenshots/rotimidesign.png)

**What it is** — A solo product-design studio portfolio — 'Srotimi' (designer Stephen) — that pitches itself, per the split hero, as a 'Design Partner For / Ambitious Tech Startups.' The promise is to take design off a founder's plate: 'You've got the idea. Great. Leave the rest to me!' It's the studio behind the morphing-halftone card the tweet was showing off.

**What it shows** — A long (~10.6k px) scrolling portfolio. After the two-line hero and the 'Leave the rest to me' value line, the page becomes a dense wall of project thumbnails — dark and light product UIs, dashboards, brand and app work — interspersed with a client testimonial ('Stephen is a great design partner who works with you to understand & deliver your brand vision… elevates and strengthens the overall brand experience').

**How it works** — Built and hosted on Framer (the framer runtime is present and every asset is served from framerusercontent.com). Analytics go to events.framer.com. The layout is a Framer image grid (77 imgs, 24 inline SVGs, a single iframe), so the whole experience is essentially a curated case-study gallery rather than an app.

**How it's designed** — Clean, monochrome and gallery-led so the work supplies the color. White ground (rgb(255,255,255)), near-black type (the h1 renders rgb(36,36,36)). Two webfonts load via Framer: Aeonik (TRIAL Regular 400) carries the headings — the h1 is Aeonik 24px/400 — and Geist Mono 400 provides the technical/mono accents, with Inter as the system fallback. Restraint in the chrome lets the dense, colorful project thumbnails do the talking.

**Palette** — White ground with near-black text (#242424); deliberately monochrome chrome so the colorful project thumbnails carry the palette.
**Fonts** — Aeonik (TRIAL Regular 400) for headings + Geist Mono 400 for accents, loaded via Framer (Inter fallback).
