# BigIdeasDB | Find Proven Problems. Build Your Next SaaS Product
> Find Proven Problems. Build Your Next Successful Startup.  ·  [bigideasdb.com](https://bigideasdb.com/)

![bigideasdb.com](../../../screenshots/bigideasdbcom.png)

**What it is** — The marketing site for BigIdeasDB, an AI platform that mines 238,000+ real user complaints (G2, Capterra, Reddit, Upwork, App Stores) into validated SaaS/startup ideas, each with revenue estimates, competition gaps and step-by-step build guides. It is one of two builder-product links carried in the Om Patel tweet — but it is NOT the Google-Maps lead-gen tool the tweet actually describes.

**What it shows** — A very long landing page: the hero 'Find Proven Problems. Build Your Next Successful Startup.', a 'What is BigIdeasDB?' explainer, stacked platform-feature sections (Integrated Intelligence, Market Intelligence, AI Research Assistant, App Store Intelligence and research-pipeline mockups), a 'You find the idea. Build it with anything.' section, a Trustpilot-backed 'Trusted by Builders and Indie Developers' wall, $149-lifetime pricing, and an FAQ.

**How it works** — A Tailwind + daisyUI site (the :root carries daisyUI's oklch theme tokens — --p, --s, --su, etc.) with InterDisplay/Inter self-hosted, a Crisp chat widget, Plausible + Google Tag Manager + Meta and Google ad pixels, a Trustpilot widget and Rewardful affiliate (r.wdfl.co). It is enormous and screenshot-dense — ~15.7k px tall on desktop with 122 images and 332 SVGs.

**How it's designed** — A bright, light SaaS aesthetic: white background with a black 60px InterDisplay H1 and daisyUI color tokens — a warm orange-red primary (--p oklch(73.5% 0.168 40)) and a green success accent (--su oklch(74.6% 0.181 152)). Cards, badges and product screenshots dominate a clean, conversion-focused layout.

**Palette** — White canvas, black headings, warm orange-red primary with green success accents (daisyUI tokens).
**Fonts** — InterDisplay (display) + Inter (body), self-hosted.
