# Flowstate - Master Flow State for Peak Performance
> Master Flow State to Sharpen Focus, Multiply Output, and Operate at Peak Performance.  ·  [flowstate.com](https://flowstate.com/)

![flowstate.com](../../../screenshots/flowstatecom.png)

**What it is** — A conversion-funnel landing page for Flowstate, a science-backed flow-state training program for founders and leaders (the 'Meet Rían' section and the description — 'Science-backed training for founders and leaders' — point to Rían Doris / the Flow Research Collective lineage). The hero pitch, verbatim, is 'Master Flow State In 30 Days.'

**What it shows** — A dark hero stating the 30-day promise, an inline name-capture step ('What's your name?') that kicks off a multi-step questionnaire, a 'FLOW TRIGGER' section, and a 'Meet Rían' founder bio block. CTAs are solid white pills.

**How it works** — A single dark scroll (~2,633px) structured as a quiz/opt-in funnel — the 'What's your name?' input begins a multi-step lead-capture flow. There is no JS framework global exposed; lead/ad tracking runs through the LinkedIn Insight Tag (snap.licdn.com) and the Facebook pixel (connect.facebook.net), with type served from Google Fonts.

**How it's designed** — Near-black ground (rendered rgb(13,13,13)) with white text, Montserrat for the big display headings and TWK Everett as the body sans (the --font-sans token), warmed by a cool blue/cyan primary accent defined in HSL (--primary hsl(207 78% 66%), --ring hsl(190 78% 67%)). The stylesheet ships a full shadcn-style token system (--background, --foreground, --primary, --chart-1…5, radius and shadow scales). Buttons are solid white pills with near-black text.

**Palette** — Near-black ground (rgb(13,13,13)), white text, white CTA pills; cool blue/cyan primary accent (hsl(207 78% 66%) / ring hsl(190 78% 67%)).
**Fonts** — Montserrat (display headings) + TWK Everett (body sans, via --font-sans); of the large declared family list only Montserrat and Space Mono actually load as webfonts.
