# Ankit Rai®
> MOLESKINE — Observations in ink  ·  [ankit-rai.com](https://www.ankit-rai.com/moleskine)

![ankit-rai.com](../../../screenshots/ankit-raicom.png)

**What it is** — The 'Moleskine' project page inside the Framer-built portfolio of Ankit Rai, a Design & Construction Technologist. It reframes his personal travel sketchbook as an interactive web exhibit, and is the page Matt (@Matthias_Oel) points to as the live home of his Flipbook 3D component.

**What it shows** — A near-black page whose pitch headline is the single word 'MOLESKINE'. The centerpiece is a 3D page-turning book of finely detailed architectural pen-and-ink sketches (US Capitol, MIT Stata Center, figure studies), flanked by italic journal copy ('As an architect, I can't help noticing corners, light, shadows…'). Below it the page continues through sections titled OBSERVATIONS IN INK, FIRST LINES, BEHIND THE LINES, TERRAINS and Reflections.

**How it works** — A long vertical scroll page (~4,950px). The hero interaction is the flipbook: arrows on either side page you through ~30 spreads with a realistic curl animation, while the prose invites you to 'slow down, look closer.' It's an image-driven 3D widget (no canvas/video — CSS-transformed images) rather than a heavy WebGL scene.

**How it's designed** — Restrained, gallery-like dark layout: a #121212 ground with white display headings set in Josefin Sans and delicate italic Lato body text (weight 300), so the monochrome ink drawings carry all the color. Built on Framer; a small smartarget widget and Framer's own analytics are the only third-party payloads.

**Palette** — Near-black ground (#121212) with white (#fff) headings and grey secondary text (#a7a7a7); a green token (#0bde66) and a red link-hover accent appear in the theme.
**Fonts** — Josefin Sans (display headings) + Lato (italic 300 body)
