# NOUS RESEARCH / JOHN GALT | Are.na
> A sample of my work with Nous Research.  ·  [are.na](https://www.are.na/john-galt/nous-research-john-galt)

![are.na](../../../screenshots/arena.png)

**What it is** — A single channel on Are.na, the research-style bookmarking/moodboard platform. This channel, 'NOUS RESEARCH / JOHN GALT', is curator john-galt's collection — self-described as 'A sample of my work with Nous Research' — and is the source the tweet credits for StudioMilitary's aesthetic.

**What it shows** — A dense masonry grid of 157 saved blocks: electric-blue dithered/halftone faces, anime and manga portraits, poppy flowers, staff-wielding wizard figures, purple 'HERMES 3' imagery and ritual-poster artwork. A left Info rail lists the description, 'Started over 2 years ago', 'Length 157', 'Followers 18', the channels it appears in (Applied Aesthetics, Brutalist Aesthetics), and a Grid/Table view toggle. The channel is marked CLOSED (read-only) and was viewed logged out.

**How it works** — Standard Are.na channel view: a breadcrumb header (Are.na / John Galt / NOUS RESEARCH / JOHN GALT) with Follow / Connect / More actions, then a lazy-loading block grid that streams in as you scroll (documentElement scrollHeight ~16,670px at 1440 wide). Clicking a block opens it; Connect adds it to your own channels.

**How it's designed** — Utilitarian Swiss/editorial chrome that gets out of the artwork's way: a plain white ground with black text and muted grey controls, the custom 'areal' grotesque for headings, and almost no decoration so the saturated blue-and-purple thumbnails carry all the color. The grid itself is the design.

**Palette** — White ground (rgb(255,255,255)), black body text, dark-grey heading (rgb(51,51,51)) and muted grey controls (rgb(105,105,105)); all color comes from the saved image blocks.
**Fonts** — Custom 'areal' grotesque for headings (with an areal fallback); body resolves to a Times serif fallback.
