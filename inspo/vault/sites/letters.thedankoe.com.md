# future/proof | DAN KOE | Substack
> stay relevant  ·  [letters.thedankoe.com](https://letters.thedankoe.com/)

![letters.thedankoe.com](../../../screenshots/lettersthedankoecom.png)

**What it is** — The custom-domain landing page for "future/proof", Dan Koe's Substack newsletter (h1 "future/proof", subtitle "stay relevant") — a personal-development / creator-economy publication with over 311,000 subscribers per the on-page count.

**What it shows** — On load the page is gated by Substack's full-screen subscribe modal: the publication's stylized line-art portrait avatar, the title, the "stay relevant" tagline, the "By DAN KOE · Over 311,000 subscribers" credit, an email capture with a green Subscribe button, and a "No thanks" dismiss. Behind it the home feed leads with the post "Purpose & Profit – A Guide To Discovering Your Life's Work".

**How it works** — Standard Substack publication template on a custom domain: an email-first conversion gate intercepts the visit, dismissible via "No thanks" to reach the chronological post list. Subscribe/read flows are Substack-hosted; analytics run through Sentry, Datadog RUM and GTM.

**How it's designed** — A dark theme (true-black #000000 web background via Substack theme vars) with a muted sage-green accent/pop color (#54675e) on the Subscribe CTA — a restrained, editorial palette rather than Substack's default orange. Typography is the classic Substack pairing: Spectral serif for body/reading and SF Pro Display / system sans for the UI and the 24px bold h1. Minimal imagery beyond the avatar; the focus is the word and the email box.

**Palette** — True-black canvas (#000000) with sage-green accent (#54675e) on the Subscribe CTA; white headings, grey reading text
**Fonts** — Spectral (serif) for reading body; SF Pro Display / system sans for UI + h1
