# The Prohuman
> Get AI tools and insights in minutes. Trusted by 700,000+ readers.  ·  [theprohuman.ai](https://www.theprohuman.ai/)

![theprohuman.ai](../../../screenshots/theprohumanai.png)

**What it is** — The beehiiv home/archive of 'The Prohuman,' a daily AI newsletter (bylines include Hasan Toor and Rimsha Bhardwaj) — pitched in meta as 'Get AI tools and insights in minutes. Trusted by 700,000+ readers from Google, Amazon, NVIDIA, Adobe, and OpenAI.' Ihtesham Ali's tweet about a 'First Principles Breakdown' Claude prompt links here.

**What it shows** — A white, magazine-style feed of recent issues — headlines such as 'AI-designed vaccine reaches human trials,' 'Gemma 4 12B comes to laptops,' 'Majorana 2 raises the quantum stakes,' 'Codex gets an AWS path' and 'Anthropic speeds up Opus releases' — each card tagged (AI stories / Breakthrough), bylined and timestamped, beneath a top nav of Sponsor and 'Subscribe (It's FREE)'.

**How it works** — beehiiv-hosted (favicon, og:image and theme tokens all point to beehiiv) and styled with beehiiv's '--wt-*' variables; no SSR JS framework global is present. Open Sans is pulled from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com) and is the only loaded webface, while the same beehiiv marketing/bot stack runs in the background — Google Tag Manager, PerimeterX (client.px-cloud.net), Birdie and Cloudflare Insights. The homepage is image-heavy (51 imgs, 19 inline SVG icons) and ~2809px tall on desktop.

**How it's designed** — A bright editorial light theme: a pure-white background with dark-grey body text (--wt-text-on-background-color #222, rendered rgb(34,34,34)), a black primary color (--wt-primary-color #000) and pale-grey card surfaces (button rgb(249,250,251)). Headlines run large (h1 at 48px) in Open Sans, and the body is a dense grid of story cards with thumbnails, topic tags and author bylines.

**Palette** — White background, dark-grey #222 text, black primary, pale-grey #f9fafb cards
**Fonts** — Open Sans (Google Fonts) — the only loaded webfont
