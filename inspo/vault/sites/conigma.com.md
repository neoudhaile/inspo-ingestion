# Join the Conigma GTM Community
> Join our GTM Community on Slack  ·  [conigma.com](https://www.conigma.com/community)

![conigma.com](../../../screenshots/conigmacom.png)

**What it is** — This is the signup gate for Conigma's go-to-market (GTM) Slack community — the landing the tweet's free Claude Co-work course routes to. The <h1> is the whole ask: "Join our GTM Community on Slack", behind a short form asking for "Your LinkedIn URL."

**What it shows** — A single-screen lead-capture page: headline, a LinkedIn-URL input, and a join CTA, gated by a Cloudflare Turnstile challenge. It's purpose-built as a conversion page for founders/agency owners requesting the GTM/Claude Co-work material, not a full marketing site.

**How it works** — Built and hosted on Webflow (assets and stylesheet from cdn.prod.website-files.com, webflow-icons font). It runs Ahrefs analytics, uses Cloudflare Turnstile for bot protection, and pulls a jQuery bundle from CloudFront — a typical Webflow form stack.

**How it's designed** — A clean, warm, conversion-first look: a cream/off-white canvas (rgb(250,247,235)) with dark grey body text (#333), a true-black primary button with white label for maximum CTA contrast, and black headings. Typography pairs Mona Sans 600 for the heading with Inter (400/500/600) for body and form — a modern, neutral SaaS pairing kept deliberately minimal.

**Palette** — Warm off-white canvas (rgb(250,247,235)) + dark-grey text (#333), black CTA button.
**Fonts** — Mona Sans 600 (heading) + Inter 400/500/600 (body & form).
