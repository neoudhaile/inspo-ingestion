# mapzilla — the data-visualisation & cartography portfolio of Craig Taylor
> 3D maps, terrain and movement data rendered as art (derived; the page ships no meta description).  ·  [mapzilla.co.uk](https://mapzilla.co.uk/)

![mapzilla.co.uk](../../../screenshots/mapzillacouk.png)

**What it is** — The portfolio of Craig Taylor ('mapzilla'), a Squarespace site with page title simply 'mapzilla' and no meta description or on-page h1/h2 — the page is a project index. The Mapbox + three.js terrain-occlusion demo in the tweet is his recent work; this site collects the broader body of it.

**What it shows** — A dense two-column grid of vivid 3D data-visualisation / cartography projects (rendered as h3 titles): 'NBA All-Star Weekend Data Visualisation', '#TransitVizThursdays | Innovative Transit Dataviz', 'Coral Cities: Updated Workflow 2023', 'XMEN Map Pin-Table', 'MTB DataViz & Analytics', 'Fractal Cities', 'Make sense of it all: Mapping Movement', 'The Global Tanaka Project', '2022 Wrapped', 'The BODS Bus Trilogy', '2021 Wrapped', 'Miniature Worlds', 'City Domes', 'Window Cities' and 'Commuter Flowers'. A slim top nav reads Projects / Tutorials / Instagram / Contact.

**How it works** — Built on Squarespace (scripts and styles from static1.squarespace.com, assets.squarespace.com and definitions.sqspcdn.com), with webfonts from Adobe Typekit (use.typekit.net) and Google Fonts. The index is static and image-heavy — 45 <img>, 15 <svg>, 0 canvas/video/iframe — over a 12,320px page; the live WebGL/three.js pieces live inside individual project pages rather than on the index.

**How it's designed** — A minimal white frame around maximalist imagery. The Squarespace theme sets --siteBackgroundColor hsla(0,0%,100%,1) and getComputedStyle confirms a white body rgb(255,255,255) with black text. There is no h1/h2 — project titles render as h3. Type is Poppins (loaded 300/500) for the UI/nav, with ITC Avant Garde Gothic Pro declared via Typekit but unloaded on the index, plus Squarespace's own UI/social icon fonts. All colour comes from the saturated project thumbnails — glowing city-light maps, coral-like data blooms, iridescent terrain and 'Commuter Flowers' — against the white gallery.

**Palette** — White canvas (--siteBackgroundColor #fff) with black text; every hit of colour comes from the saturated 3D map / data-viz thumbnails.
**Fonts** — Poppins (loaded 300/500) for UI and nav; ITC Avant Garde Gothic Pro declared via Adobe Typekit but unloaded on the index; plus Squarespace's UI and social-icon fonts.
