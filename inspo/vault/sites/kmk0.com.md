# Xavier (Jack) – kmk0 – @KMkota0
> Making websites is my passion. Here's a quick view to what I am working on and links to relevant projects.  ·  [kmk0.com](https://kmk0.com/)

![kmk0.com](../../../screenshots/kmk0com.png)

**What it is** — The personal portfolio of Xavier (Jack) / @KMkota0 — author of the motion-graphics tweet and maker of mo¹. The site is a playful index of the web apps and experiments he is building.

**What it shows** — A bento grid of project cards: Desktop.fm ('Virtual Hangout for Music Lovers', with a spinning CD/disc render), ThreeTools (a Three.js editor, orange Y-logo), MiniTAP (a 'Fun Sequencer' styled like a fitness band), Wiggle Bones (a glossy balloon-dog 3D render), a 'Videos' card with three thumbnails, and a 'Captures' icon grid. A blue diagonal-stripe banner caps the top; the footer shows 'Xavier Jack @kmkota0' with a Follow button.

**How it works** — A single-screen bento layout (no scroll at desktop; scrollHeight == 900) where each card is an outbound link with an 'Open external link' affordance to the live project (three.tools, minitap.io, desktop.fm, wiggle.three.tools) plus social links (Twitter, YouTube, TikTok, Instagram).

**How it's designed** — Friendly, OS-widget aesthetic: a light grey ground with rounded, frosted/elevated cards, a blue diagonal-stripe header, and a single blue accent (#3b84e3, with a faded #3b84e388 variant). Glossy 3D product renders are the visual hero. Built on Nuxt with Tailwind utility classes, and it ships no webfont — type is the Tailwind system-sans stack.

**Palette** — Light grey/white ground, black text, blue accent (#3b84e3) carried by the striped banner and links.
**Fonts** — System sans only (Tailwind ui-sans-serif / system-ui stack) — no webfont payload.
