# Contra Labs — a frontier human data & evaluation lab for creative AI
> A frontier human data and evaluation lab making AI better for creativity.  ·  [contralabs.com](https://contralabs.com/)

![contralabs.com](../../../screenshots/contralabscom.png)

**What it is** — Contra Labs (from Contra, the professional network for independent creatives) is a 'frontier human data and evaluation lab' for creative AI — fronting 1.5M+ verified creative experts who benchmark style, tone and taste for AI models and applications. It's the site the bookmarked tweet singles out for its renaissance-oil-painting-meets-dithered-ASCII visual identity.

**What it shows** — A long (~13,900px) single-page site. The hero headline 'A frontier human data and evaluation lab making AI better for creativity.' sits with Request-partnership / Research CTAs above a scrolling logo wall of the AI and no-code tools it works with (Framer, Lovable, HeyGen, Ideogram, Glide, Kajabi, Jitter, Kittl and dozens more). Below come sections 'Meet the ContraLabs ecosystem', 'Leading next-gen creative AI research.', 'Trusted by frontier labs and applications.', and a closing manifesto pairing 'Execution is free.' with 'Now, judgment is everything.'

**How it works** — Built on Framer — assets served from framerusercontent.com with events.framer.com telemetry and Segment (cdn.segment.com) analytics, plus one unpkg-hosted stylesheet. It is media-dense (6 canvas, 4 video, 19 img, 9 svg) which drives the animated Renaissance imagery and the ordered-dither/halftone panels. No Next/Nuxt/React-root signature was exposed (Framer's own runtime).

**How it's designed** — An editorial, gallery-grade aesthetic. From the DOM probe the canvas is a warm off-white (body background rgb(251,250,246) ≈ #FBFAF6) with near-black ink (h1 color rgb(43,43,41)). The display type is Source Serif 4 Variable (the h1 renders at 50px/400 in 'Source Serif 4 Variable', serif) paired with the GT Standard L grotesque (Regular 400 / Medium 500 / Semibold 600 all report loaded) for UI and body; Inter is declared in the stylesheet but reports unloaded. The signature move is compositing classical oil-painting portraits with black-and-white dithered/halftone rectangles that stand in for the laptop and tablet screens the figures hold.

**Palette** — Warm off-white canvas #FBFAF6 with near-black ink (rgb(43,43,41)); the color energy comes from the Renaissance oil imagery (golds, deep reds, teal).
**Fonts** — Source Serif 4 Variable for the serif display headings; GT Standard L (Regular/Medium/Semibold) grotesque for UI and body; Inter declared but unloaded.
