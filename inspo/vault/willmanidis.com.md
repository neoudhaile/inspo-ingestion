# Will Manidis
> I live in New York. I founded ScienceIO, a language model provider, which we sold to a large EMR provider.  ·  [willmanidis.com](https://willmanidis.com/)

![willmanidis.com](../../screenshots/willmanidiscom.png)

## What it is
A deliberately minimal single-screen personal homepage for Will Manidis, founder of ScienceIO (sold to a large EMR provider) and now an angel investor. The whole site is one quiet bio card; the only heading is his name.

## What it shows
Three short paragraphs of first-person bio on a near-white page: living in New York; founding and selling ScienceIO; “walking and investing” with early rounds in Brain Co, Chroma, Thatch, Silna and others; and inline links to his writing and photos. The page holds a single image and two small SVGs — no hero art, no marketing.

## How it works
There is no scroll — the document height equals the viewport (single screen). A small top “Home” nav sits above the bio, and the prose carries inline outbound links: ScienceIO, the Veradigm acquisition press release, LinkedIn, plus internal links to writing and photos. The experience is read-and-leave.

## How it's designed
Classic understated personal-site restraint, built on Squarespace (assets.squarespace.com, sqspcdn). The ground is a warm off-white (rgb(252,252,252)) with dark-grey body copy (rgb(51,51,51)) set in Helvetica Neue, while the “Will Manidis” wordmark is Futura PT 700 at 42px. Fonts come from Adobe Typekit (use.typekit.net): futura-pt, proxima-nova, and adobe-garamond-pro are all loaded. Links use a muted teal-blue (rgb(41,95,124)). It's a text-first card with no imagery doing the talking — typography and whitespace are the whole design.
