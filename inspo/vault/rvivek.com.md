## What it is
The personal essay archive of Vivek Ravisankar (co-founder/CEO of HackerRank), a stripped-back writing blog whose homepage is just a reverse-chronological index of posts going back to 2014.

## What it shows
A simple top nav (Me / Writing / Speaking) over a dated list of ~50+ short essays on startups, hiring, leadership and life — 'Leadership', 'Startup Learnings', 'Building strong teams', 'Raising the bar', 'SaaS', 'Work life balance', 'Market Size', 'Angel vs VC', and so on. No images, no hero, no cards — pure index.

## How it works
A static, content-only site (no JS framework globals, zero images/canvas/video/svg, single same-origin stylesheet) with Adobe Typekit pulled in for webfonts. The full homepage is one ~1.3k px screen — essentially a hand-rolled or Jekyll-style blog index.

## How it's designed
Classic editorial minimalism: white background, pure-black body text in Open Sans, with Inconsolata (Typekit) for monospace accents and a single dark-red rgb(170,0,0) link color as the only chrome. No CSS custom properties, no design system — typography and whitespace do all the work.
