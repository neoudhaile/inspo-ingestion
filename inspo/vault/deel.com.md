## What it is
The marketing homepage for Deel, the all-in-one global HR platform — its own pitch is the h1 "Hire, manage, pay, & equip anyone, anywhere." and the meta line "Hire, pay, and manage teams in 150+ countries." It sells global payroll, compliance, EOR, contractor management and HRIS as a single product.

## What it shows
A deep-violet hero with a phone mockup of the app, a logo wall of customer brands, then a section-by-section product tour organized around the h2s "The Global People Platform", "Accomplish more in less time", "One modern experience for today's workforce" and "Our customer reviews", closing on big proof-point stats (150+ countries, +40,000 customers, $20B+, 90+) and a testimonial grid.

## How it works
Long single-scroll landing page (~7,700px) with a sticky top nav whose product menus expand on hover; it's a content/CMS-driven marketing site rather than an interactive app, so the experience is scroll-through storytelling toward repeated "Book a demo / Get started" CTAs. Heavy instrumentation (HubSpot forms, Amplitude, GTM, a Navattic interactive demo embed) drives lead capture.

## How it's designed
Confident SaaS brand system: a violet hero anchors an otherwise white, near-black-text canvas, with the custom Bagoss type family (Condensed for the big display h1 at 60px/weight 500, Standard + Extended elsewhere) giving the headlines a distinctive editorial voice while Inter carries body copy. Dense iconography (495 inline SVGs) and product screenshots fill the modular card sections; a couple of canvas elements power animated accents.
