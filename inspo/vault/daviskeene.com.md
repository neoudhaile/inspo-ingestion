# Home | Davis Keene
> Davis Keene's personal website — engineer, software developer, creator  ·  [daviskeene.com](https://daviskeene.com/)

![daviskeene.com](../../screenshots/daviskeene.png)

## What it is
The personal homepage of Davis Keene, an engineer at Handshake. A deliberately minimal, text-first 'about me' page that he's been incrementally making 'more fun' with small interactive flourishes (the subject of his 'making my personal site more fun' posts).

## What it shows
A centered single column on near-white. Up top: a geometric 'K' monogram (two black triangles) with a slim nav — More / Projects / Photos / Blog — and a moon icon for dark mode. Below it, a black-and-white self-portrait sliced into offset, scattered rectangles (a glitchy photo-collage effect), then a warm first-person intro: 'Happy Thursday! I'm Davis, an engineer, software developer, creator, twin, illini, rock climber, coffee drinker…' with inline underlined links to Handshake, UIUC and a résumé. Footer is three social glyphs (Twitter, GitHub, LinkedIn).

## How it works
A plain vertical page you read top-to-bottom; navigate via the underlined inline links or the top nav to Projects / Photos / Blog. The main interactive control is a light/dark theme toggle (moon icon). The personality lives in micro-interactions — chiefly the fragmented portrait — rather than in heavy motion or layout tricks; it's the 'fun' experiment grafted onto an otherwise classic résumé page.

## How it's designed
Restrained editorial minimalism: generous whitespace, a single centered measure of text set in Libre Franklin, near-black on off-white. No decorative color, almost no chrome — the lone visual play is the sliced grayscale self-portrait, which gives a standard personal page a quirky, hand-made feel. Tiny footprint (4 images, a handful of SVG social icons, one button), no canvas or video.

- **Palette:** Off-white ground (#fafafa), near-black text (#000) with grey secondary (#666) — essentially monochrome, plus a dark-mode variant via the toggle.
