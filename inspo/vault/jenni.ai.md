## What it is
A SaaS marketing landing page for Jenni, an AI academic-writing and research assistant aimed at students, researchers, and academics. The hero pitch is literal — the real <h1> reads “Meet Your Intelligent Research Assistant” — and the page exists to convert visitors into sign-ups for the writing app.

## What it shows
A long, image-heavy scroll (~13,200px) selling the product: a hero, a numbered “From blank page to cited paper in three steps” (01/02/03) walkthrough, feature blocks (“Built for serious research writing,” “Not another AI chatbot,” “Catch weaknesses before reviewers do”), journal social proof (“Our users have published papers in 100+ journals”), collaboration and use-case sections, a changelog, tutorials, an FAQ, and a closing CTA. The page carries 45 images, 153 inline SVG icons, and 10 iframes (embedded product demos/loops).

## How it works
Standard top-to-bottom marketing scroll with Framer-driven motion and embedded demo iframes. The three numbered steps anchor the core narrative, and repeated CTAs push toward starting in the app. No auth is needed to browse; the whole funnel is one page.

## How it's designed
Built on Framer (assets served from framerusercontent.com, with events.framer.com analytics). It's a clean, bright marketing aesthetic: white body ground (rgb(255,255,255)) with near-black ink, headings rendered in deep slate (rgb(16,24,40)). Type is Inter-led — the hero <h1> is Inter weight 500 at 62px — with Be Vietnam Pro 600 and Fragment Mono as loaded accent faces. The most striking thing under the hood is the marketing/measurement stack: Microsoft Clarity, Meta Pixel, LinkedIn Insight, Bing UET, Google Tag Manager/Ads, an OpenAI ads pixel, and Cookiebot consent all fire on load.
