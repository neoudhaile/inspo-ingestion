# IgnytLabs — Build and Validate in 21 days
> From concept to clickable product. We handle strategy, design, and launch so you can focus on building momentum.  ·  [ignytlabs.com](https://www.ignytlabs.com/)

![ignytlabs.com](../../screenshots/ignytlabscom.png)

## What it is
Marketing site for IgnytLabs, an MVP studio (founder Prajwal Tomar) that promises to build and validate a product in 21 days — the h1 reads 'Build and Validate in 21 days' and the meta pitch is 'From concept to clickable product.' It is the tweet author's own studio, linked alongside the CLAUDE.md write-up.

## What it shows
A long single-scroll page: hero with the 21-day promise and trust badges (MVP in 21 days / AI-Driven Development / Scalable Tech Stack), a 'Move at Startup Speed' section, a '3x Faster Than Traditional Teams' 21-vs-100-days comparison bar, MVP case-study cards (Learn Daily, Nenic, CredBoost, a Multimodal AI Chat Assistant), a 'Landing Pages That Actually Convert' section, client testimonials, an 'All your questions, answered' FAQ, and a closing 'Ready to Ignite Your Idea?' CTA.

## How it works
A ~13,800px Framer marketing scroll with repeated 'Book a slot' CTAs that all route to a cal.com/prajwal-tomar/book booking page; one embedded video and a couple of canvases add motion to the otherwise content-card flow.

## How it's designed
Glossy SaaS-agency look on a white canvas (rgb 255,255,255) with soft blue blurred gradient blooms, a deep-navy hero token (#0e0520), and a blue accent ramp peaking at #0099ff; near-black ink headings (rgb 3,30,46). Type pairs Geist for UI/body with Poppins for display and an Inter fallback. Built on Framer (framerusercontent assets, Framer Events analytics).
