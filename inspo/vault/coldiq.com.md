# ColdIQ - Tomorrow's GTM Systems Built for You Today.

## What it is
The homepage of ColdIQ, a B2B go-to-market agency that builds 'revenue engines' — outbound, ads and content systems powered by the latest GTM/AI tooling. It's a long, proof-heavy sales page aimed at getting growth teams to book a call.

## What it shows
A broadsheet-length landing page led by the serif headline 'Tomorrow's GTM Systems Built Today.' over a hero video. Below it stacks the full agency playbook: a 'GTM Teams that trust ColdIQ' logo strip, '3 ways we help you grow — content, ads & outbound', a demand-gen funnel diagram, a wall of testimonials ('Don't take our word for it — Hear it from them'), a 'Where we shine' capabilities grid citing 500+ GTM teams, a 'Your results timeline — what happens after onboarding' section, a featured pull-quote ('ColdIQ became our #1 outbound growth engine...'), an 'Official partners of ColdIQ' tool-logo row, an FAQ and a 'Let's Get Started!' CTA, closing on a fat footer of Growth / Resources / GTM Tools / Free Tools links.

## How it works
A Webflow-built lead funnel: every section funnels toward booking/contact CTAs, social proof (client logos, testimonials, partner badges) does the persuading, and a free-tools/resources footer captures top-of-funnel SEO traffic. Under the hood it's wired with an unusually dense visitor-intelligence and martech stack — PostHog, Hotjar, Sentry, GTM and a stack of B2B lead-deanonymization/tracking pixels (Koala, Albacross, HappierLeads, Identity Matrix, Leadsy, Factors, Warmly and more) — plus Wistia for video.

## How it's designed
Warm, premium-agency editorial rather than cold SaaS. A soft off-white/cream ground (--off-white-500-neo #fffbf5) carries a near-black warm ink (--brand-black-neo #2c2825) with an electric blue accent (--blue-600-f-main #0b7bfa) for links and highlights. The headline voice is a Flechatests display serif (h1 64px / weight 400) set against a Geist sans body (with Geist Mono for labels), a pairing that reads expensive and modern. A meticulous Webflow design-token system (margins, radii, type scale all as CSS vars) underpins the layout, with heavy use of logos, screenshots and embedded video.
