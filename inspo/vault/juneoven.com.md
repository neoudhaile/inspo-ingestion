# June Oven: 12-in-1 Countertop Convection Oven
> I'm a full countertop smart kitchen with sizzle to spare.  ·  [juneoven.com](https://juneoven.com/)

![juneoven.com](../../screenshots/juneovencom.png)

## What it is
The Shopify storefront/product page for the June Oven, a 12-in-1 smart countertop convection oven (now under Weber). It appears in Garry Tan's tweet as the demo target — a real product whose site/app stands in for “a website with no public API / community fan project” that Printing Press can turn into an agent CLI or skill so an agent setup can drive the oven. June's own copy leans into the bit: “Proving daily that entire kitchens can be built out of computer code.”

## What it shows
A bright white storefront (rendered body white, grey body text rgb 107,110,123, slate-ink headings rgb 29,37,45). It's a media-saturated scrolly product page: a hero (“I'm a full countertop smart kitchen with sizzle to spare.”) then a grid of its twelve modes as h2s — Convection Oven, Air Fryer, Grill, Roaster, Slow Cooker, Dehydrator, Toaster, Reheat, Pizza Oven, Proofer, Broiler, Warming Drawer — plus “June App Membership Tiers,” “Hi, I'm June.,” an FAQ, and “June thinks like a chef and cooks like one too.” It is extremely rich: 31 video, 51 img, 12 canvas, 71 svg, 4 iframe.

## How it works
A long Shopify scroll (~10133px) with a sticky header, cart drawer and an on-load newsletter popup; checkout runs through Shopify/shop.app, support through Zendesk and Weber consumer care, and there are companion iOS/Android apps. The page is heavily instrumented — Klaviyo, Trustpilot, Signifyd, Meta Pixel, Google Tag Manager, Bing and Rakuten tags, with Vimeo serving the videos.

## How it's designed
Bright, premium appliance e-commerce on white with a hot-orange CTA (--button-background-rgb 255,90,60, sale price #ff5a3c) against slate-ink headings (#1d252d). Type is 'Euclid Circular' (400–700) for headings and body, with 'Publico Headline' serif loaded for editorial moments (Rogliano/Calibre/Jost/Poppins linger in the stack). It's a Shopify theme (window.Shopify present, cdn.shopify.com assets, --heading/--text-font-family set to Euclid Circular). The craft signal is sheer volume of motion — 31 inline videos and 12 canvases demoing cooking — carrying the story over UI chrome.
