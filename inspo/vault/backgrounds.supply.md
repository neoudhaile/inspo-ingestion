# Jaw-dropping backgrounds for your projects
> A huge collection of handcrafted and AI-Generated Backgrounds  ·  [backgrounds.supply](https://www.backgrounds.supply/)

![backgrounds.supply](../../screenshots/backgroundssupply.png)

## What it is
A paid pack/store of background assets — 'a huge collection of handcrafted and AI-Generated Backgrounds' — by Kailash (@kail_designs), the tweet's own author. The h1 is 'Jaw-dropping backgrounds for your projects.'

## What it shows
A long dark gallery of hundreds of background previews (267 <img>), animated WebGL shader backgrounds (4 <canvas>, powered by Unicorn.studio), and named packs (Chromatica, '19 Backgrounds', Sereno) with 'Get Now' / 'Unlock Now' buttons.

## How it works
A long Framer-built, Framer-hosted site (~10,067px; framerusercontent.com + events.framer.com) with a large lazy-loaded image grid, embedded Unicorn.studio interactive shaders, and LemonSqueezy checkout links for the paid packs. Analytics run through Google Tag Manager and Hotjar.

## How it's designed
A bold dark showcase: a black ground with white Satoshi display headings (h1 rgb(255,255,255)) that lets the saturated, glossy background thumbnails and live shaders supply all the colour. Type is the typical Framer set — Satoshi for display, plus Geist, Outfit, Inter and an 'Editor's Note' italic serif accent. It is image-heavy by design (267 images and 4 live shader canvases).
