# Home
> WE MAKE GOOD BRANDS GO BAD  ·  [badmarketing.com](https://www.badmarketing.com/home-x)

![badmarketing.com](../../screenshots/badmarketingcom.png)

## What it is
The marketing site for BAD Marketing ("Bold And Disruptive"), a full-service performance/growth agency formed as "4Media x Hemon" that the tweet credits with $20M/yr in agency revenue. The pitch headline is a literal play on the brand: 'WE MAKE GOOD BRANDS GO BAD'.

## What it shows
A long (10.8k px) dark sales page that opens on the GO BAD hero, then runs the agency playbook: a logo wall of 'leading growth channels' partners, a 'Don't hire marketers, hire a team of growth experts' value section, big animated stat counters ($ / $ / + figures), service buckets for Ecommerce Growth, Coaching/Info Product and Local Business, an embedded 'message from our founder' video, and a cookie-consent layer.

## How it works
A Webflow-published static site. It leans on Webflow's interactions for hover/scroll animation and a Swiper carousel, embeds 7 videos and ~114 images, and is wrapped in a heavy marketing/analytics stack — Google Tag Manager, the full HubSpot suite (analytics, ads pixel, collected-forms, banners), Microsoft Clarity, Twitter and Facebook pixels, and Termly consent.

## How it's designed
Aggressive dark-mode agency look: near-black #141414 canvas with off-white #f4f4f4 body text and a punchy red #ee4250 primary button. Typography is set in URW Nimbus Sans Extended (loaded weights Light/Regular/Bold/Black), with the h1 at a loud 56px in the Bold cut. The CSS defines a wide branded variable palette (sandy-brown #ffa84b, A2-orange #fe9a02, plum #cd64bf, vivid-green #06a77d, bright-red #ee4250) used as section accents across the scroll.
