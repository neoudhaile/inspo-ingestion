# Vercel: Build and deploy the best web experiences with the AI Cloud

## What it is
The marketing home of Vercel, the deploy/hosting platform that created and maintains Next.js. Its own <h1> pitches it as 'Build and deploy on the AI Cloud.'

## What it shows
A long (≈5,900px desktop) scrolling landing page that walks through Framework-Defined Infrastructure, Fluid Compute, the AI Gateway, and a 'Deploy your first app in seconds' CTA, dense with product diagrams and logo proof — the probe counts 50 images, 111 inline SVGs and a canvas-driven hero animation.

## How it works
Self-hosted Next.js app (App Router — no legacy __NEXT_DATA__ blob) rendered on Vercel itself; every script and stylesheet is served from vercel.com with no third-party CDNs in the head.

## How it's designed
Textbook Geist Design System: the :root exposes the full --ds-* and --geist-* token scale (grays, the signature --geist-success #0070f3 blue, a 4px-based spacing grid, radius tokens). Body renders near-black text rgb(23,23,23) on an off-white rgb(250,250,250) field; the Geist and Geist Mono webfonts are loaded (KaTeX and DSEG7 faces are declared but stay unloaded). Crisp, high-contrast, restrained — color is reserved for status accents.
