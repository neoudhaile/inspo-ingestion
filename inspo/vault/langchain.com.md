# LangChain Careers

## What it is
The careers page of LangChain, the company behind the widely-used LangChain / LangGraph agent-building frameworks. It is a recruiting landing page that sells the team and culture, then embeds the live job board.

## What it shows
A hero ('We're a growing team of builders making an outsized impact in our industry') over a flowing line-art motif, an 'Our operating principles' four-up grid (build something great, embrace humility, maximum agency, run to the rescue), a 'Backed by the best in the business' investor-logo row (IVP, Benchmark, Sequoia), an 'Explore our job opportunities' section powered by an Ashby embed, an awards/recognition strip (Enterprise Tech 30, IA40, InfraRed 100, Forbes AI 50), and a large LangChain wordmark in the footer.

## How it works
A Webflow page (~5.7k px) with scroll reveals; the live roles are pulled in from an Ashby job board (jobs.ashbyhq.com) via iframe, and a HubSpot/Segment stack drives newsletter signup and tracking. CTAs route to the embedded openings.

## How it's designed
Deep-navy near-black canvas (rgb(3,7,16)) with pale-blue text (rgb(204,233,255)), a light-weight 72px TWK Lausanne display headline, Aeonik for body, and JetBrains Mono for technical accents — a calm, premium, agent-infra house style. Generative flowing-line artwork in the hero supplies the only strong imagery; otherwise it leans on tight typography and a restrained blue/violet token system.
